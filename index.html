About MIC Armenia

Microsoft Innovation Center Armenia

The Microsoft Innovation Center Armenia (MIC Armenia) was founded in May of 2011 and joined the global network of MICs to support local IT communities to expand, strengthen innovation and improve competitiveness. In partnership with top IT companies in Armenia, MIC Armenia organizes different training courses, coding bootcamps and certification courses.

Since 2021, MIC Armenia has led the way in advancing cybersecurity education in Armenia through our partnership with EIF Cybersecurity Initiative — CyHub Armenia. Together, we've created and offered specialized training courses and events that equip professionals with the skills to defend against modern cyber threats.

Our efforts not only aim to enhance cybersecurity expertise but also to promote cyber awareness, ultimately contributing to a safer digital environment in Armenia and beyond.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

My child is 8. Can they still join? ▼

The course is designed for children aged 9–13. If your child turns 9 soon or is particularly mature for their age, please contact us at mic@mic.am and we will assess together.

What if my child has never used a computer? ▼

No prior tech experience needed. The course is accessible to all children aged 9–13 regardless of background. Participants work in teams, so no child is ever left behind.

Will the course be in Armenian? ▼

Yes. All instruction, activities, and materials are in Armenian. Key cybersecurity terms are introduced alongside clear Armenian explanations.

What will my child take home? ▼

Every child receives an official MIC Armenia × CyHub Armenia Cyber Hero Certificate and practical skills they will use every day online. Many also take home a Day 1 mission card as a take-home challenge.
